Dr. G. Venkata Subbaiah

Dr. G. Venkata Subbaiah, Associate Professor, Department of Mechanical Engineering, M.V.S.R Engineering College, Nadergul, Hyderabad has completed B. Tech in Mechanical Engineering from REC, Warangal (presently NIT) in 1994. M. Tech in Mechanical Engineering in the specialization of "Refrigeration & Air Conditioning" from JNTU, Anantapur in 1997 and Ph.D in Mechanical Engineering with research topic as "An investigation to improve the performance of the diesel engine by using bioadditives" from JNTU Anantapur in May 2012.
Dr. Venkata Subbaiah has teaching experience of 17 years in premier institutions such as SASTRA Deemed University, Tanjavur, KITS Warangal, Vasavi College of Engineering Hyderabad and M.V.S.R Engineering College, Hyderabad. He has published 13 papers in national and international journals and 06 papers in national and international conferences. He is a Life member of ISTE, State managing committee member of ISTE Andhra Pradesh Section for 2 terms (January 2006-December 2011), Executive Council Member of ISTE New Delhi (January 2012-December 2014 and also for the present January2015- December2017
