Dr. G. Chakraverti

Dr. G. Chakraverti, Professor and Director (R & D), obtained Bachelor-s degree from O.U College of Engineering, Hyd. He obtainedM.Tech from IIT, Delhi and Ph.D. from University of Roorkee, Roorkee (Presently IIT, Roorkee) in Mechanical Engineering. He was the Principal of JNT University College of Engineering, Hyderabad. He has 45 Years of teaching experience and more than 30 years of research experience .He has published more than 50 research papers in National & International Journals & conferences. He guided 4 Ph.Ds. He is the recipient of Khosla Research award from University of Roorkee, merit certificate from Institution of Engineers (India) and best teacher award from Govt. of Andhra Pradesh. He is presently working as Professor of Mechanical Engineering & Director (R&D;) at Mahaveer Institute of Science&Technology;, Hyderabad.
